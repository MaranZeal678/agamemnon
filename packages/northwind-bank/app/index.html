Data Protection

—

Northwind's account records are held behind Agamemnon, a write-path guardrail. This application has no direct database access — every read and every deletion is served and checked by Agamemnon. If Agamemnon is offline, records cannot be viewed or changed.

Small deletesrun immediately
Over 100 recordsheld for a supervisor's approval
Over 2,500, or 20× normalblocked outright
Anything deletedcan be undone from a snapshot

To review held or blocked actions, open the Agamemnon app.
